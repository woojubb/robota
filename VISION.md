# Vision — Our agents develop and advance our agents

**Our agents develop and advance our agents.**

We build a dependable, adaptable foundation on which our agents can develop their own system and
grow through that work. Each improvement should strengthen their ability to carry the next
improvement themselves. We pursue this continuing cycle with reliability worthy of a system
entrusted to change itself.

Stable layers, multipurpose capability and autonomous development serve this purpose. Their forms
can evolve as models, tools and needs change. The direction endures: our agents become increasingly
capable of carrying their own development forward.

## The question to return to

> **Does this work help our agents carry their own development forward more capably and reliably?**

This question guides judgment. Foundational work can contribute indirectly and over time. Continuous
change alone is not advancement, and no fixed image of a final perfect agent defines the destination.

## Why this direction

Development can strengthen the agent that carries subsequent development. This is the opportunity
we pursue; useful compounding must be demonstrated through actual outcomes. The system must earn
the trust required to change itself, preserving working capability as it advances.

Our agents developing this system is central to the purpose. Reusable mechanisms and competence
beyond this repository keep that development useful as future requirements emerge. Common layers
remain neutral, with task and product choices composed at their proper boundaries.

This is an ambition to build toward, not a claim that reliable autonomous self-evolution is already
achieved. Current capabilities and guarantees belong in their technical documentation. Operational
developer guidance belongs in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and [HARNESS.md](HARNESS.md).
