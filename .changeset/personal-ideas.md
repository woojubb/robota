`@robota-sdk/agent-product/personal` adds ideas. `IdeaBook` keeps thoughts for later, each with tags and marked as the person's own or as the agent's suggestion. Nothing acts on an idea until the person takes it up, and an idea taken up records what it became. It has file and memory stores behind one port, and `IdeaAdd` and `IdeaList` tools.
