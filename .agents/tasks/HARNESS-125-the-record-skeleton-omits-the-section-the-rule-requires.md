# HARNESS-125: the record skeleton omits the section the rule requires

## Objective

`backlog-execution.md` requires every record to carry `## User Execution Test Scenarios` — a real
scenario, or `Not applicable` with the reason written. Neither thing that PRODUCES a record emits it:

```
.agents/templates/spec-template.md          grep -c 'User Execution' -> 0   (across its 10 `## ` headings)
scripts/harness/allocate-work-item-id.mjs   :219 ## Objective   :223 ## Plan   — and nothing else
```

An author who fills in the sections they were given produces a non-compliant record. Emit the section
from both producers, in the form the rule requires.

Source: https://github.com/woojubb/robota/issues/2308

## Plan

- [ ] Add the section to `.agents/templates/spec-template.md` in the rule's required form.
- [ ] Add it to `allocate-work-item-id.mjs`'s emitted skeleton.
- [ ] Emit the required CHOICE, not a bare heading — a skeleton that emits an empty section makes every
      new record pass `scan-spec-user-execution-section` while saying nothing.
- [ ] Red-proof: a record generated by the skeleton satisfies the scan for the right reason, and a
      record with the heading but no choice does not.
- [ ] Verify: the scan and its suite, plus the harness scan set.
