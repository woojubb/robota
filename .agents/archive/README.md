# Archive

Retired artefact kinds, kept because they are history and deleting them would lose it. Nothing writes
here, and no live process reads it.

A directory belongs here when its documents are no longer produced and no longer acted on — not
merely when their work is finished. Finished work stays with its kind: a completed Task lives in
`.agents/tasks/completed/`, a closed spec-doc in `.agents/spec-docs/done/`.

## What is here

- **`task-breakdowns/`** — 422 pre-PROC-006 work breakdowns: checkbox plans, no frontmatter, one per
  unit of work, written when `.agents/tasks/` meant something different from what it means now. They
  overlap 102 Task IDs, because the same work often had both a backlog item and a breakdown. Kept
  whole rather than merged into the Task tree, since they are a different document kind and merging
  would have made 102 IDs resolve to two documents of different shapes.

Scans treat this tree the way they treat every other frozen record — a defunct package name or a
superseded term here is history, not drift.
